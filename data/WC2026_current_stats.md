# World Cup 2026 — Live Goalkeeper Stats (as of 2026-06-21)

Group-stage goalkeeper statistics for the current tournament, compiled from match
reports (ESPN, Sofascore, FIFA match centre, Opta/The Analyst, Sky Sports, Al Jazeera).
The tournament is mid-group-stage: most teams have played 1–2 of their 3 group games.

> **Data caveat:** automated page fetches to the major stats providers are 403-blocked in
> this environment, so figures come from cross-checked search extracts of those same pages.
> Match scores, clean sheets, goals conceded and headline save counts (Vozinha 7, Room 15,
> Al-Owais 9) are well corroborated. A few per-match *save totals* were not published as
> discrete numbers and are flagged `approx`. Nothing was fabricated; unfound values are noted.
> `goals_prevented` is the Opta/Sofascore post-shot metric where it was published.

## Headline storylines
- **Vozinha (Cape Verde), 40** — 7 saves and a clean sheet to hold Spain 0-0 on debut; Sofascore
  9.7 (Player of the Match), +1.46 goals prevented. The story of the group stage.
- **Eloy Room (Curaçao), 37** — **15 saves vs Ecuador** (tied Tim Howard's 2014 single-match WC
  record) for the smallest nation ever to qualify; a perfect 10.0 rating, +2.48 goals prevented.
- **Mohammed Al-Owais (Saudi Arabia)** — tournament-leading **9 saves** in a 1-1 vs Uruguay.
  (He is the actual starter; pre-tournament presumed #1 Nawaf Al-Aqidi is injured and has not played.)
- **Raúl Rangel (Mexico)** — two clean sheets in two games; Mexico first to clinch the Round of 32.
